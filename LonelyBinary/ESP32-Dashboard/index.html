Help

GPIO Safe mode and Expert mode

Safe mode is the default. Only the configured board allowlist can be toggled. Every other GPIO is displayed as locked, and the Python API rejects requests for it.

Expert mode temporarily unlocks GPIO0, GPIO43, and GPIO44 until you relock them or restart the board. GPIO0 is sampled during reset to select the boot mode. GPIO43 and GPIO44 are normally UART0 TX and RX, so using them may interrupt serial logs, the REPL, or UART firmware uploads.

Critical pins remain locked in both modes: GPIO19–20 are normally USB; GPIO22–25 do not exist on the ESP32-S3; GPIO26–37 may be connected to flash or PSRAM; GPIO45–46 are strapping pins; and GPIO48 is reserved for the onboard RGB LED.

Expert unlock is permission—not electrical verification. Confirm every pin against the exact PinPulse board and module schematic before connecting hardware or driving an output.

Bluetooth and OUI

The ESP32 scans passive BLE advertisements. Public addresses may match the imported IEEE database. Random/private BLE addresses often rotate and should not be treated as a stable identity.
